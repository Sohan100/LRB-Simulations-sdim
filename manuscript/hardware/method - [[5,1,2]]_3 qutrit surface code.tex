\subsection{The \texorpdfstring{$[[5,1,2]]_3$}{[[5,1,2]]3} Code}
\label{sub:code}\label{sec: error detection code}

% Sec. 2.2 (track hw). The device subsubsection moved to "hardware placeholder.tex" (hw version only); the conjugation table and the encoding circuit moved to Appendix B ("appendix - check rounds and noise.tex").
The logical qutrit is encoded in five data qutrits $q_0, \dots, q_4$ by the $[[5,1,2]]_3$ code, a distance-two patch of the qutrit surface code~\cite{Moussa2016}. Its code space is the joint $+1$ eigenspace of four stabilizer generators of weight three, two of $Z$ type and two of $X$ type,
\begin{align}
  G_1 &= Z_0 Z_1^{-1} Z_3^{-1}, & G_2 &= Z_1 Z_2 Z_4^{-1}, \label{eq:s1}\\
  G_3 &= X_0 X_1 X_2^{-1}, & G_4 &= X_1^{-1} X_3 X_4^{-1} .
  \label{eq:s4}
\end{align}
The measured eigenvalues of the four generators form the syndrome, which is trivial when all four are $+1$. Because the distance is $d = 2$, every one-qutrit Pauli error gives a nontrivial syndrome, but the syndrome does not identify the error. The code therefore detects errors and cannot correct them. Its logical Pauli operators are
\begin{equation}
  \bar Z = Z_0 Z_2, \qquad
  \bar X = X_0 X_3 .
  \label{eq:logicalPauli}
\end{equation}

The logical Clifford gates are transversal in the sense of the folded surface code~\cite{Moussa2016}. They consist of one-qutrit gates and of two-qutrit gates within the pairs of qutrits that the fold of this construction places on top of each other (\autoref{app:background}), and the five-qutrit patch has one such pair. The single-qutrit logical Clifford group is generated by
\begin{align}
  \bar H &=
  \left( H_0 H_1^{-1} H_2 H_3 H_4 \right) \mathrm{SWAP}_{2,3}, \label{eq:logical_H}\\
  \bar S &= S_0\, S_1^{-1}\, \mathrm{CZ}_{2,3}\, S_4 .
  \label{eq:logical_HS}
\end{align}
Both generators map the stabilizer group to itself and act on $\bar X$ and $\bar Z$ as $H$ and $S$ act on $X$ and $Z$ (\autoref{app:rounds}). Every logical Clifford is a word in $\bar H$, $\bar S$ and the logical Paulis $\bar X$ and $\bar Z$, which are products of one-qutrit gates (\autoref{sec:clifford-gen}). The logical Clifford gates therefore need a single two-qutrit coupling, between $q_2$ and $q_3$.

The pair $q_2$, $q_3$ results from exchanging the roles of $q_1$ and $q_2$ in the definitions that the folded-code construction of~\cite{Moussa2016} gives for this patch (\autoref{fig: 5-qutrit-code}). There the pair is $q_1$ and $q_3$, two opposite corners of the patch, and the two-qutrit gate becomes local only through the folding step, which needs additional ancillas and SWAP gates. With our definitions the pair is the central qutrit and a corner, which are diagonal neighbors on the patch of \autoref{fig: 5-qutrit-code}, so this patch needs no folding step.
