% Qubit noise channels: sdim 1.4.0 (N1 'd' uniform over the q^2 - 1 non-identity Paulis, N1 'f' over the q - 1 nonzero shifts, N2 uniform over the q^4 - 1 non-identity Paulis) and CIRCUIT_LEVEL_NOISE_RATES in src/lrb/circuit_generator.py, identical in the qubit-lrbd-sdim140 and conventional-sites worktrees.
To separate the role of the local dimension, we repeat the simulations for the qubit $[[5,1,2]]$ code, with $q = 2$ in place of $q = 3$. Its stabilizers and logical Paulis are those of Eqs.~\eqref{eq:s1}--\eqref{eq:logicalPauli} with every exponent taken modulo two, so that $G_1 = Z_0 Z_1 Z_3$, $G_2 = Z_1 Z_2 Z_4$, $G_3 = X_0 X_1 X_2$ and $G_4 = X_1 X_3 X_4$. The gates $\bar H$ and $\bar S$ keep the form of Eqs.~\eqref{eq:logical_H} and~\eqref{eq:logical_HS}, with $H^{-1} = H$ and the qubit phase gate $S = \mathrm{diag}(1, i)$, CNOT takes the place of SUM, and the logical Cliffords are drawn from the $24$-element single-qubit Clifford group, whose words have $2.54$ primitive gates on average (\autoref{sec:clifford-gen}). The noise models keep the multiples of $p$ of \autoref{tab:circuit-noise}. DEP1 applies $X$, $Z$ or $XZ$ and DEP2 one of the $15$ non-identity two-qubit Paulis, each chosen uniformly, and a flip applies $X$. With $\omega = -1$ the estimate of Eq.~\eqref{eq:estimator} becomes $\langle \bar{X} \rangle_s = P_0 - P_1$, and the error rate of Eq.~\eqref{eq:error_rate} becomes $r = (1 - f)/2$. The check rounds keep their expected numbers of faults, and a logical Clifford, whose words are shorter, puts $2.8p$ on the data qubits under SI1000 and $14.5p$ under SD6. The layouts, check rounds, postselection, mirror inverse, baseline and fits are those of the qutrit code.

Only the treatment of the region near the pseudo-thresholds differs, in two points. The search for the crossing drops two conditions of the qutrit search, a tolerance and an error floor, which are comparable to the qubit error rates at the crossings under SI1000 (\autoref{app:campaign}). The qubit pseudo-thresholds also lie below the range of the high-$p$ extension, so refinement runs on a finer grid of $p$ take its place (\autoref{tab:runs}).
