# Does the Dorito chip go in the paper?

Opened 2026-11-24 by Sohan. Status: waiting for Chuanhong's review of the figure.

## The question

At the meeting we leaned toward leaving the Dorito chip out, given how much material the paper
already has, and deferred the decision until Chuanhong sees the regenerated figure.

## The figure and what backs it

- `layouts/folded-surface-code/dorito-chip.pdf`: the distance-5 chip with the distance-3 and
  distance-2 codes it contains outlined. The distance-2 code is the paper's `[[5,1,2]]` code with
  the paper's labels.
- `layouts/folded-surface-code/layout-note.pdf`: the construction for every distance, what the
  fabrication has to provide (two coupler lengths, five couplers on most data qubits, one kind of
  crossover, three frequency groups) and the comparison with Moussa's folding.
- `layouts/folded-surface-code/memory/README.md`: memory under SI1000 and SD6 against the rotated
  surface code, and the cost of the fold-transversal H and S.

If it goes in, it supports the layout message (angle 2 in `main-message.md`): the paper's code is
the bottom of a family that scales on superconducting hardware with nearest and next-nearest
neighbour couplers only.
