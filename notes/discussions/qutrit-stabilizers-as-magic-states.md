# Qutrit stabilizer states as qubit magic states

Opened 2026-11-24 by Sohan, from the meeting (`../meetings/2026-11-24-chuanhong.md`). Status: open,
speculative.

## The idea

A high-fidelity qutrit stabilizer state, teleported onto a qubit, becomes a magic state: the
`omega` and `omega^2` phases of the qutrit stabilizer formalism are non-Clifford for qubits. If that
works, protected qutrit stabilizer states could be a cheaper source of qubit magic states.

## Open points

- Which qutrit stabilizer states map to which qubit magic states, and through what interface
  between a qutrit and a qubit?
- What fidelity would the qutrit state need, and how does that compare with magic state distillation
  or cultivation on qubits?
- Has anyone shown this already?
